# Design Brief — Slaag

_Generated by /design-grill on 2026-11-17. Update this file when design decisions change._

> **Scope.** This brief sets the visual direction of the **Slaag brand** (brand #1). Slaag is a multi-brand platform (PRD AC-B1): the *structure* here — two registers, semantic tokens, feedback rules, motion rules — applies to every brand; the *values* (colours, typeface, mascot, tone) are Slaag's and live in the brand configuration (`packages/brands/slaag.json`, see [TECH-STACK.md](docs/TECH-STACK.md)). No component may hard-code them.

## Product Context
- **Category:** Consumer learning app (test & exam prep, Afrikaans, Grades 4–7) with a parent oversight app. **One brand, two registers:**
  - **Child register** — prep sessions, feedback, celebrations, credits, badges, streaks.
  - **Parent register** — dashboard, test planner, alerts, account & subscription, landing/campaign pages.
  - **Back office** — out of scope for design effort: stock Mantine with the brand primary colour only.
- **Target users:**
  - **Child — "Anja", 10, Grade 5**, Afrikaans/dual-medium school in a town like Potchefstroom. Hand-me-down Android phone or family tablet (cracked screen, low storage). Does a session around 16:00, sometimes reluctantly. Reads fluently but skims; taps fast; a wrong answer stings; closes anything that feels like homework. **Design for the top of the range too:** a Grade 7 (13) child must never find it babyish.
  - **Parent — "Marelize", 41**, working mother, often an AfriForum/Solidariteit member. Checks Slaag on her phone in short bursts (school pick-up, after a push alert); laptop only for sign-up and billing. Moderately technical (WhatsApp, banking apps). Worried whether her child is ready — wants "are we on track?" answered at a glance, not charts.
- **Primary device/context:** Phone first for both (Android app + mobile web on iPhone); laptop for parent sign-up/billing. 2 GB RAM Android phones, < 5 MB data per 15-minute session.
- **Defining screen:** the **child's prep-session question screen**, including the answer-feedback moment. Second: the **parent dashboard readiness card**.

## Brand Personality
**5 adjectives:** encouraging, unflappable, clever, warm, cheeky
- *Encouraging* — never shames; always shows the way forward (AC-G2).
- *Unflappable* — calm under exam pressure; last-minute mode says "we've got this", not "panic".
- *Clever* — knows the textbook and what's likely to be tested; credible to parents.
- *Warm* — Afrikaans, family-minded; a favourite tannie who happens to be a teacher.
- *Cheeky* — a little humour so it's not a school system; this is what a 13-year-old tolerates.

**Emotional goal:** Child → **capable** ("I can do this, and I'm getting better"). Parent → **reassured** ("I know where we stand, and it's handled").

**Tone:** The cool older cousin doing well at university who helps you study at the kitchen table — not a teacher, not a cartoon. Afrikaans identity shows through language, humour, mascot, local references and the green-and-orange colour family; no flags or heritage symbols.

## Visual References
- **Headspace** — warm flat illustration, rounded friendly shapes, calm colours with personality. Model for "unflappable + warm".
- **Monzo** — plain-language status at a glance, warm colour on a calm trustworthy structure. Model for the parent dashboard.
- **Brawl Stars / Clash Royale menus** — chunky, tactile, pressable buttons; reward reveals and level-ups. "Cool, not babyish." Used sparingly: celebration and badge screens and the child's primary buttons only.
- **Duolingo** — borrow only the *speed* of its feedback loop, not its look.
- **Spotify Wrapped** — bold "look what you did" summary moments (end of session, end of test).
- **AfriForum** (afriforum.co.za) — colour-family reference only: Afrikaner association with green and orange (their `#00723A` / `#13612E` green, `#F58220` orange, Montserrat).

## What to Avoid
- **School portals / Siyavula-style platforms** — functional blue-and-grey, curriculum codes on the surface ("CAPS Gr5 T2 NS 3.2"), progress tables, clip-art. The child sees "your test on Friday".
- **Mathletics / IXL-style drill apps** — cartoon overload (babyish at Grade 7), skill grids with percentages, feeling scored everywhere.
- **Duolingo's look** — saturated lime green, its owl-like mascot, guilt-trip notifications. Copying it makes Slaag look like a knock-off.
- **Looking like a partner's product** — campaign pages put Slaag beside AfriForum, Solidariteit and OUTA logos; Slaag uses the same colour *family* but its own shades and typeface, never AfriForum's exact colours or Montserrat.
- **The real competitor** is the parent quizzing from the textbook the night before plus the class WhatsApp group: Slaag must feel calmer and less effort than that.

## Color System
- **Default mode:** Light (dark mode later — see below).
- **Temperature:** Warm. Neutrals carry a faint warm/green tint; never cold blue-grey.
- **Palette approach:** Two brand colours with a clear split.
  - **Green = primary** — structure, navigation, primary buttons, "on track", correct answers.
  - **Orange = energy accent** — celebrations, credits, streaks, highlights, partial ("almost") answers.
  - Parent register is green-led; child register turns up the orange.
- **Saturation:** Rich, not neon — deep teal-leaning emerald and a warm tangerine.
- **Starting point** (provisional — `/uicolor` refines; contrast checked against background `#FFFBF5`):

  | Token (semantic) | Value | Contrast | Use |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | `primary` | `#0E7A5A` | 5.16:1 on bg; white on it 5.32:1 | Buttons, links, nav, "on track", correct |
  | `accent` | `#FF8A3D` | 2.27:1 — **fills only** | Celebration, credits, streak, partial fill |
  | `on-accent` | `#1F2A24` | 6.33:1 on accent | Text/icons on orange — **never white on orange** |
  | `accent-text` | `#B4540F` | 4.83:1 on bg | Orange used as text |
  | `background` | `#FFFBF5` | — | Warm off-white page |
  | `text` | `#1F2A24` | 14.4:1 on bg | Body text (green-tinted near-black) |

- **Feedback colours (child):** correct → green; partial ("amper") → orange + what was right + the missing key point; wrong → **neutral** colour + encouraging line + model answer. **No red for wrong answers.** Never colour alone — every state also has an icon and words.
- **Red** is reserved for genuine problems only (suspended account, failed payment, destructive confirmations).
- **Progress** always fills up; never counts down.

## Typography
- **Typeface:** **Nunito** (open licence, variable 400–900) for everything — rounded terminals match "warm/cheeky" without being cartoonish; open letterforms for young readers; full Afrikaans coverage (ê ë ô û î ï, 'n). Distinct from AfriForum's Montserrat. Self-hosted on web and bundled in the Android app; subset to Latin to protect the data budget.
- **Heading/body:** Same family; personality comes from weight.
  - Child: headings/questions 800–900, large; body ≥ 18px; generous line height; one question or idea per screen.
  - Parent: headings 600–700; body 16px; compact but uncrowded.
- **Scale approach:** Child spacious; parent balanced.

## Component Style
| | Child register | Parent register |
|---|---|---|
| **Corner radius** | 16px cards; **pill** buttons and answer choices | 12px cards; 10px buttons |
| **Elevation** | **Tactile:** primary buttons and answer choices have a solid darker bottom edge (2–4px) that compresses on press | **Subtle:** soft warm-tinted shadows on overlays (modals, menus) only; cards flat with borders |
| **Density** | Spacious; tap targets ≥ 56px; one focus per screen | Balanced; targets ≥ 44px; summary first, details one tap away |
| **Borders** | Mostly borderless; shape from colour fills | Light 1px warm-grey |

**Overall:** soft and tactile for the child, calm and structured for the parent — same shapes, colours and type. Pressable buttons stay distinct from Duolingo through colour, typeface and restrained bounce.

## Animation
- **Level:** Child — **expressive at key moments only**. Parent — **subtle**.
- **What moves (child):** answer feedback, progress fill, credits counting up, badge/level unlocks, end-of-session celebration (AC-G3), mascot reactions.
- **What moves (parent):** 150–200ms fades/slides for sheets and modals, readiness bars filling on load; nothing else.
- **Timing:** feedback 150–250ms; celebrations 1.5–3s and tap-to-skip; parent 150–200ms.
- **Easing:** ease-out entrances; small overshoot **only** on child celebrations and button presses; no bouncy page transitions; parent ease-out only.
- **Key interaction to make feel great — the post-answer moment** (repeats ~30× per session): tap/submit → button compresses → feedback within ~200ms. Correct: green fill, small burst, +credit. Partial: orange, what they got right, the missing key point. Wrong: neutral, encouraging line, model answer slides up.
- **Performance (2 GB phones, AC-G7):** animate transform and opacity only — never layout or blur. Celebrations are vector/code animations, never video or GIF (< 5 MB per session). `prefers-reduced-motion` / system animation scale → everything becomes fades.

## Dark Mode
- **Add later.** Light only for the January 2027 pilot and February 2027 launch. Every colour is a **semantic token from day one** so dark mode is a second value set in the brand file, not a refactor. First candidate after launch (parents checking in at night).

## Decisions Made
| Decision | What was decided | Why |
|----------|-----------------|-----|
| Surfaces | One brand, two registers (child / parent); back office out of scope | Children are seen on parents' phones — must feel like one product; parent app must not feel like a toy |
| Personas | Anja (10, Gr 5, old Android) and Marelize (41, phone in bursts) | Concrete targets for density, tone and device |
| Age ceiling | Design must hold up for a Grade 7 (13-year-old) | Older children reject babyish design |
| Personality | Encouraging, unflappable, clever, warm, cheeky | Older-cousin tone, not teacher or cartoon |
| Emotion | Child: capable. Parent: reassured | Replace night-before dread and uncertainty |
| Cultural identity | Shown through language, humour, mascot, local references and green/orange; no heritage symbols | Afrikaner association without looking political |
| Colour | Green primary + orange accent, Slaag's own shades | Afrikaner green/orange association, distinct from AfriForum and Duolingo |
| Feedback colours | Correct green, partial orange, wrong neutral; red only for real problems | Mistakes must be cheap (AC-G2) |
| Typeface | Nunito, one family | Warm, readable for Grade 4, Afrikaans coverage, small payload |
| Components | Child soft/tactile/pill; parent calm/bordered/12px | Two registers, one system |
| Motion | Child expressive at key moments; parent subtle; transform/opacity only | Delight within 2 GB / 5 MB budgets |
| Dark mode | Later; semantic tokens now | Pilot deadline; no rework later |
| Defining screen | Child question screen, then parent readiness card | Where "capable" and "reassured" are won |

## Ruled Out
_Directions explicitly rejected during this session. Do not re-propose these without reopening the decision._

| Direction | Why it was rejected |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Separate visual world for children | One brand, two registers — children's screens appear on parents' phones |
| Designing the back office | Out of scope; stock Mantine + brand primary |
| School-portal look (blue/grey, tables, curriculum codes, clip-art) | Opposite of "capable"; it's what children dread |
| Cartoon-heavy drill-app look | Babyish for Grade 6–7 |
| Duolingo's look (lime green, owl-like mascot, guilt-trip nudges) | Reads as a knock-off; guilt contradicts "encouraging" |
| AfriForum's exact colours or Montserrat | Would look like an AfriForum product beside other partners |
| Red for wrong answers | Shaming; contradicts AC-G2 and "capable" |
| White text on orange | Fails contrast (2.35:1) |
| Separate display font (e.g. Fredoka) for child screens | Babyish risk at Grade 7; extra bytes |
| Timers, scores or countdowns on the question screen | Makes it feel like a test |
| Dark mode at launch | Pilot deadline; daylight child usage |
| Video/GIF celebrations | Data budget and 2 GB performance |
| Mascot B "Speletjie" (thick outline, sticker look) | Risks looking babyish for Grade 7 and sits close to Duolingo's territory |
| Mascot C "Die neef" (green scarf, pencil) | Scarf disappears on green screens; accessories lost at icon size |

## Open Questions
- **Mascot name clearance** — check "Snippie" for existing South African trademarks and app-store names before launch. Credits could become "neute" (nuts) Snippie stores for test day.
- **Mascot final art** — the chosen direction exists only as a rough concept sketch; final art and its animated moods need an illustrator.
- **Exact colour values** — provisional; `/uicolor` to build the full ramps (green, orange, warm neutrals) and confirm against real screens.
- **Last-minute mode tone** — should celebrations be quieter in last-minute sessions to stay "unflappable"? Decide during `/uiux` on the question screen.
- **Partner campaign pages** — how much of the partner's branding (logo only, or also their colour) appears on co-branded landing pages.
